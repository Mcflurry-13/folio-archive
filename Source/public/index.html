Connect RedNote in Chrome

Install the connector once. After that, pasted RedNote links are read automatically. Use Folio and RedNote in the same Chrome browser.

  1. Turn on Developer modeOpen chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  2. Load the connectorClick Load unpacked and choose the browser-extension folder in the Folio project.
  3. Sign in to RedNoteOpen xiaohongshu.com and sign in, then refresh Folio on port 5180 and paste a full share link.

The connector returns only the note you ask for, to local Folio. Cookies are never read or exported. A background tab opens briefly during capture. For video notes only the cover is attempted.

The connector allows only local port 5180, so other previews show Not connected.

About the Folio agent

  1. Bring saves inImport RedNote Markdown, links, a Folio backup or an image pack. Your library stays in this browser.
  2. Organize with suggestionsAI proposes topics with its reasons. Nothing moves until you accept.
  3. Use it in any taskDescribe your task. Folio searches saved text and your notes; it does not analyze images or video.
  4. Continue in your coding toolSelect references and export a Markdown brief or JSON pack. The CLI reads that pack only; it does not connect to your live browser library.

Ground rules

  • Cites sourcesAI drafts are suggestions. Open the references to check their source text and links before using them.
  • Says when nothing fitsIf no saves match, Folio tells you and suggests a wider scope.
  • Asks before guessingAdd missing facts in the conversation and review any flagged borrowed text before export.
  • Only what you savedFolio reads your saves and changes nothing on the original platforms. It never reads your network or copies AI chat history.
