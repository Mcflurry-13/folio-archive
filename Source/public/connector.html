连接小红书 Connect RedNote

安装一次连接器，之后粘贴链接即可读取正文和配图。请在同一个 Chrome 浏览器中打开 Folio 并登录小红书。

Install the connector once, then paste links to capture text and images. Open Folio and sign in to RedNote in the same Chrome browser.

  1. 打开 chrome://extensions，开启开发者模式。

    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

  2. 点击“加载已解压的扩展程序”，选择项目里的 browser-extension 文件夹。

    Click Load unpacked and choose the project’s browser-extension folder.

  3. 打开 小红书 并完成登录。刷新 Folio，粘贴完整分享链接。

    Open RedNote and sign in. Refresh Folio and paste a full share link.

连接器会做什么 What the connector does

连接器只向本机 Folio 返回你指定笔记的正文与图片，不读取或导出 Cookie。读取时会临时打开一个后台笔记标签页。需要登录、验证或无法访问的笔记会提示重试。视频笔记只尝试读取封面，不下载视频文件。

The connector returns the requested note to local Folio only. It does not read or export cookies. A temporary background tab opens during capture. Notes that need sign-in or verification, or are unavailable, ask you to retry. For video notes only the cover is attempted; video files are not downloaded.

连接器只允许本机 5180 端口。5181 设计预览会显示“未连接”，这是正常的。当前聊天内置浏览器不能安装此 Chrome 扩展；已有收藏可先导出 JSON，再在 Chrome 中导入。

The connector only allows local port 5180, so the 5181 design preview shows “not connected” as expected. The built-in chat browser cannot install this Chrome extension; export your materials as JSON and import them in Chrome.
